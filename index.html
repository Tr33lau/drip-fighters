How to play

Moves

Punch / KickFast jab, stronger kick. Punch chains into kick on hit.
Down + PunchLow jab.
Down + KickSweep. Hits low and knocks down.
Jump + P or KAir attack. Must be blocked standing.
SpecialSP button, or ↓ ↘ → + Punch. Cancels from a punch or kick that connects. 4 per round, and it recharges for 1.5 seconds after each use.
SuperWhen the gold chain is full. Fills as you hit and get hit.
BlockHold away from your opponent. Hold down-back to block sweeps.

Touch

Left stickWalk, jump (up), crouch (down), block (back).
Right buttonsP, K, SP, SUPER.

Controller (PS5, Xbox & others)

FightingLeft stick or D-pad: move · Square punch · Triangle or Cross kick · Circle special · R1/R2 super · Options pause
MenusD-pad or stick to move · Cross select · Circle back
2 PlayersFirst controller is Player 1, second is Player 2
